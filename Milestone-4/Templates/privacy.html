Privacy policy

How information is handled

These sections describe the kinds of information the application may process and the practical considerations for using it.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include your username, email address, age or occupation if requested, and entries you add for finances, study activity, fitness, or goals. Please provide only information needed to use the application.

2. How information may be used

The application may use submitted entries to display your dashboard, organise records, calculate summaries, track goals, and generate forecasts or insights that are part of the available features.

3. Storage and retention

Information may be stored in the application's configured database. In a local or demo installation, this may be an SQLite database. How long information remains available depends on the installation, its configuration, backups, and any deletion procedures implemented.

4. AI assistant and third-party services

If the AI assistant is enabled and configured to use an external AI provider, the text you submit to that feature may be sent to that provider for processing. Avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in prompts. Check the relevant provider's privacy terms for its handling practices.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The application may use session cookies or browser storage for functions such as login sessions or interface preferences. The exact behaviour depends on the application's configuration and the browser you use. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although some features may then stop working as expected.

6. Sharing and disclosure

The application should only share information where needed to run a feature, comply with an applicable legal obligation, address a security issue, or where you have otherwise authorised the sharing. Review the deployed application's actual integrations and settings to confirm how information is shared.

7. Security and account safety

Take care to protect your login credentials and sign out when using a shared device. No website or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not upload information that you would not want processed by the configured application services.

8. Your choices

You can choose what information to enter into optional fields and features. If you need to access, correct, or request deletion of information associated with your account, use the Contact page to ask the project administrator what options are available.

9. Children's privacy

This project is not specifically designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information and it should be reviewed or removed, contact the project administrator.

10. Updates to this policy

This page may be revised as the project, its features, or its integrations change. The updated date above indicates the latest revision. Please review it periodically, especially after major changes to the application.

Project note: This is a general privacy-policy template, not legal advice or a guarantee of the application's current data practices. Before public deployment, confirm it against your actual database, session settings, hosting environment, AI integrations, analytics, and deletion process, and adapt it to applicable requirements.

Questions about your information?

Contact the project administrator if you want to ask how information is handled or what options may be available for your account.
